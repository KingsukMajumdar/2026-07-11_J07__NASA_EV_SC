\paragraph{Statistical validation}
To establish statistical confidence in the comparative
results, each algorithm is executed over 30~independent
runs using distinct random seeds (1000--1029) on Case~4
(four decision variables, $N_\mathrm{iter}=100$,
$N_\mathrm{pop}=15$).
Table~\ref{tab:stats30} summarises the TAC statistics.
GWO achieves the lowest mean TAC (\$20,682.64\,\$/yr, SD~=~\$10.97\,\$/yr,
CV~=~0.053\%), confirming its consistency.
The Friedman rank test across all 30~runs yields
$\chi^{2}=79.240$, $p<0.0001$ ($\alpha=0.05$),
with GWO achieving the lowest mean rank (1.00).
Pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank tests (one-sided, $\alpha=0.05$)
confirm GWO outperforms all three competitors
significantly (all $p<0.0001$),
with GWO achieving the lowest mean rank (1.00).
SC bank sizing shows algorithm-dependent consistency:
GWO selects 17~modules in 90\% of runs
and ARO in 50\%, while
BBO and AO exhibit higher SC-sizing scatter,
reflecting their larger TAC variability.
The best-found TAC spread in a single run is only
0.33\% (Table~\ref{tab:sizing}), demonstrating that
all algorithms locate the same near-optimal region.
Across 30~independent runs, mean TAC spans
\$704/yr
(3.40\%),
indicating that GWO and ARO provide substantially
more reliable convergence than BBO and AO for this
mixed-integer sizing problem.
